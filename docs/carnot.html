Units with k_B = 1; each reservoir is the open chain of d sites, levels −2 cos(kπ/(d+1)). The unitaries permute the joint energy levels (one to three swaps): every state stays diagonal and the numbers are exact. An engine takes heat from the hot chain (Q_h > 0) and gives out work (W > 0). With T_h ≤ T_c the bound is W ≤ 0: no engine exists. "One bath" applies every unitary to a single chain at T_h; its energy never goes down.
